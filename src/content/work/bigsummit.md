A flagship event is a brand test with an audience and a start time. Every
surface is seen in sequence, at wildly different sizes, by people who are
also in the room with each other.

I designed the visual identity for BigSummit and its application across the
physical and digital experience, working with Brand, Marketing, Web and
event production to keep the room and the run-up telling the same story.
