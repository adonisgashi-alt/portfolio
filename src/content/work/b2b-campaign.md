B2B content gets produced quickly and consumed slowly. A buyer behavior
report or a customer case study has to survive being downloaded, skimmed,
forwarded and eventually read by someone who wasn't in the original
conversation.

I art-directed and designed this body of work — reports and ebooks, case
study layouts, and the landing pages and campaign assets that carried them —
across a single editorial system rather than as a series of one-offs.
