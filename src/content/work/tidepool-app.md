## Context

Tidepool's data was accurate but its app felt clinical, and most users
stopped opening it after the first fortnight.

## Approach

**Soft data visualisation.** Charts were redrawn with rounded forms and a
restrained palette so a bad night reads as information, not judgement.

**An illustration system.** A modular set of shapes and textures lets the
team produce on-brand illustrations without an illustrator on every ticket.

**Motion with a purpose.** Transitions follow a single easing curve and
three durations, documented alongside the components.

## Outcome

Day-30 retention more than doubled, and the illustration kit is now reused by
marketing for campaigns.
