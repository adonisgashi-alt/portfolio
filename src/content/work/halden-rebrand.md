## Context

Halden had grown quickly on referral alone, but its visual identity was a
patchwork of templates. Research showed users trusted the product far more
than the brand suggested.

## The challenge

Make the brand feel as calm and dependable as the product — without losing
the warmth that drove word of mouth — and roll it out across 60+ screens in
one release.

## Approach

**Positioning first.** Three workshops with leadership reduced a long list of
adjectives to one line the whole team could design against: *money, made
quiet*.

**A restrained palette.** One soft blue carries the brand; everything else is
neutral. Colour is reserved for meaning — balance up, payment due — so it
never has to shout.

**Type as the voice.** A single grotesk with tightened tracking at display
sizes gives headlines confidence while keeping body copy friendly.

**Systemised from day one.** Every decision shipped as a token, so the rebrand
landed in product code as a theme swap rather than a rewrite.

## Outcome

The new identity launched alongside a redesigned onboarding flow. Store
conversion rose, support tickets about "is this legit?" dropped to near zero,
and the design team now ships brand-consistent work without a review
bottleneck.
