Banking apps accumulate. Every new feature earns a card on the home screen,
and after a few years the most important number on the page is competing
with a promotion for a product you already have.

This was a visual refresh, not a rebuild — same information architecture,
same engineering constraints. The work was in hierarchy, type and restraint:
deciding what deserves the top of the screen, and giving everything else
permission to be smaller.
