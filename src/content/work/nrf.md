NRF is where retail decides who it's talking to that year. The design job is
compression: one idea that survives a crowded floor, and enough depth behind
it to hold a real conversation once someone stops.

I handled visual design for the presence — stand graphics, screen content,
collateral and the campaign around it — working with Brand, Marketing and
event production from early concept through what actually got built.
