The brand had good bones and no consistency. Five business lines were
producing work in parallel, each with a defensible reason for the exception
they'd made, and the cumulative effect was a brand that felt different in
every room.

I led the visual design of a single system to replace all of it: identity,
type, color, photography, motion and layout, documented once and published
where the people who needed it already worked. The point was never
uniformity for its own sake — it was to make the right choice the easy one.
