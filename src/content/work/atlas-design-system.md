## Context

Four product teams had each grown their own UI kit. The same button existed
in eleven variations and dark mode was a per-team side project.

## The challenge

Converge on one system without freezing roadmaps — and make it something
teams wanted to adopt rather than something they were told to.

## Approach

**Audit in public.** A shared inventory of every component made duplication
visible and gave teams a stake in the outcome.

**Tokens as the contract.** Colour, type, spacing and motion became
three-tier tokens (primitive → semantic → component), so themes and brands
could change without touching components.

**Docs that answer real questions.** Every component page leads with "when to
use / when not to", then code. Search analytics steered what we wrote next.

## Outcome

Adoption hit 92% within two quarters. New features now start from composed
patterns instead of blank frames, and dark mode shipped to all products in a
single sprint.
