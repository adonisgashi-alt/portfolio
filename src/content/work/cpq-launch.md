Product marketing design sits between two audiences: the person who needs
the promise in five seconds, and the person who needs the mechanics before
they'll bring it to a buying committee.

For the CPQ launch I designed the product page and the launch system around
it, working with Product Marketing, Web and Product to get the interface
represented accurately while still reading as a clear, single argument.
