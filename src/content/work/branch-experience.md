Physical space is unforgiving: a sign that reads poorly can't be patched
after launch, and every temporary fix stays up for years.

This project was mostly editing. The design work — type scale, contrast,
mounting heights — took weeks; deciding which forty messages could become
twelve took months, and made all the difference.
