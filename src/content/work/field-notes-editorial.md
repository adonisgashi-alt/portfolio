## Context

A small editorial team wanted a magazine that felt collectible — something
readers keep on the shelf.

## Approach

**A flexible 12-column grid** that supports long reads, photo essays and
data spreads without each issue feeling templated.

**A cover system** built on one photograph, one headline and a colour pulled
from the image, giving the series a recognisable spine.

## Outcome

Four issues shipped on a tight quarterly schedule, with the fourth shortlisted
for an editorial design award.
