Annual reports are where design discipline usually goes to die: hundreds of
numbers, dozens of contributors, and a deadline that makes consistency the
first thing to be traded away.

The fix was structural. One grid, one chart language, one type scale, agreed
before a single page was laid out — which turned the last two weeks of
production from a redesign into a proofread.
