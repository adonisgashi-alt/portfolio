Commerce brought a portfolio of brands under one name, and the visual
language had to stretch to cover all of it — parent brand, products and the
audiences that only ever meet one of them.

I worked on the visual side of that transition: how the identity behaves
across web, campaign and event surfaces, and how teams across Brand, Web,
Product Marketing and UX apply it without needing a designer in the room for
every decision.
