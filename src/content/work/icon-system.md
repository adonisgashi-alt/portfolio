Icon sets fail slowly. One rushed addition at the wrong stroke weight, then
another, and within a year the set looks like it came from three different
places.

The goal here was a library durable enough to be added to by people who
weren't in the room when it was drawn — which meant the rules had to be
simple enough to hold in your head.
