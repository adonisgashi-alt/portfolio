The campaign brief asked for warmth without sentimentality — a hard balance
in financial services, where the default is a stock photo of relief.

I art-directed the photography and designed the layout system that carried
it across every placement, working from a single rule: if the image needs a
caption to make you feel something, it isn't the right image.
