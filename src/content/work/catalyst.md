Designing for developers means most of the usual marketing moves work
against you. The audience is fluent, skeptical, and looking for specifics
they can evaluate in about a minute.

I worked on the visual design for Catalyst's public surfaces — the product
site, how technical content is presented, and the launch assets around it —
keeping it recognizably part of the portfolio while sounding like it was
made by people who write code.
